About Ukash Limited

Credit should be clear, not clever

We serve individuals and small businesses across the Democratic Republic of Congo who need a straightforward path to funding.

Ukash Limited was built on a simple idea: borrowing shouldn't require a lawyer to understand. Every rate, term, and fee is disclosed upfront, before you sign anything.

We serve individuals and small businesses across the Democratic Republic of Congo who need a straightforward path to funding — whether that's covering an emergency, growing a business, or financing an asset — without the wait times or opacity of traditional banks.

Our team reviews every application personally — we don't rely on opaque scoring algorithms that reject good customers for the wrong reasons.
